Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed the manuscript "Low-profile SIW slot array with characteristic-mode-guided decoupling for 28 GHz 5G mmWave front ends: an open-source hybrid FDTD/MoM design environment" for consideration in AEU – International Journal of Electronics and Communications.

The manuscript presents (i) a dual-port substrate-integrated-waveguide slot array for the 28 GHz band whose two closely spaced sub-arrays are decoupled by resonant slits dimensioned with characteristic-mode analysis, and (ii) the complete open-source electromagnetic environment (FDTD, RWG method of moments, characteristic-mode eigensolver, SIW and Elliott synthesis) with which the design was carried out, validated against analytical references. All code and data accompany the submission so that every figure can be regenerated.

The work has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. The author declares no conflict of interest.
